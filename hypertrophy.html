HYPERTROPHY PRINCIPLES

Every set taken to 0–2 reps from failure. Progressive overload every session. Rep ranges 8–15 for maximum hypertrophy stimulus. 90 sec rest for isolations, 2–3 min for compounds. Deload every 6–8 weeks — drop volume 40%, keep intensity. Aesthetics over athletics: lats, side delts, upper chest, outer quads, tricep long head.

REST — THURSDAY

Active Recovery · Adaptation · Nutrition

Full rest day Muscle grows here

ACTIVE RECOVERY

20–30 min low-intensity walk. Light yoga or mobility work. Foam roll: IT band, thoracic spine, lats, hip flexors. Heart rate stays below 130 BPM — nothing that taxes the nervous system.

SLEEP PROTOCOL

8–9 hours minimum. Growth hormone peaks in slow-wave sleep — this is when you grow. Sleep deprivation reduces muscle protein synthesis by up to 18%. No screen time 1 hour before bed.

NUTRITION TODAY

Maintain protein at 0.8–1g per lb bodyweight. Reduce carbs slightly on rest days (–15%). Prioritize micronutrients: leafy greens, zinc, magnesium, omega-3. This is a recovery tool, not a cheat day.

HYDRATION

3–4 liters of water. Add electrolytes if you sweat heavily during training. Dehydration as little as 2% reduces strength output by 10–15%. Creatine monohydrate 5g/day — any time.

NUTRITION ESSENTIALS FOR HYPERTROPHY

Protein

0.8–1g per pound of bodyweight every single day. Distribute across 3–5 meals. Chicken, eggs, beef, fish, Greek yogurt. This is the rate-limiting factor — no program rescues a protein deficit.

Calories

Eat at a modest surplus of 200–350 calories above maintenance. Aggressive bulks build fat, not extra muscle. Slow surplus = mostly muscle, minimal fat. Track for at least 4 weeks before adjusting.

Carbohydrates

Your primary training fuel. Eat the majority of your carbs around your workouts — 1–2 hours before and within 2 hours after. Rice, oats, potatoes, fruit. Carbs are not the enemy.

Sleep

7–9 hours. Growth hormone peaks in slow-wave sleep. Sleep deprivation reduces muscle protein synthesis by up to 18% — no supplement compensates for this. Sleep is the most underrated anabolic tool.
