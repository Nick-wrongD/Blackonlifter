DAY 1 — LOWER BODY

Acceleration & Squat Focus · Quads · Glutes · Hamstrings

5 Exercises 55–65 min Explosive + Strength

Goal: Build explosive jump power and massive quad/glute strength.

Start with box jumps at 100% effort before you fatigue. These aren't cardio — they're power training. Every rep should leave you feeling the electricity of maximum output.

01

Box Jumps

3 sets · 5 reps · Maximum Power
Explosive
Jump as high and as hard as you can. Don't do these for cardio — this is pure power development.
Step down gently after each jump to protect your joints. Reset fully between reps.
Rest 60–90 seconds between sets at full recovery.
Sets3
Reps5 (max height)
Rest60–90 sec
MusclesQuads, Glutes, Calves
02

Barbell Back Squat (or Goblet Squat if brand new)

4 sets · 6–8 reps · Heavy
Main Strength
Control the weight on the way down (3 seconds), then explode back up. Own the movement.
Knees track over toes, chest stays tall, core braced like you're about to take a punch.
Sets4
Reps6–8
Rest2–3 min
MusclesQuads, Glutes, Hamstrings, Core
03

Dumbbell Walking Lunges

3 sets · 10–12 reps per leg · Dumbbells
Hypertrophy
Keep your torso upright. Front knee stays behind toes. Back knee lightly taps the floor.
Add weight once you can do 12 reps per leg with perfect form and zero wobble.
Sets3
Reps10–12 per leg
Rest90 sec
MusclesQuads, Glutes, Balance
04

Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs)

3 sets · 8–10 reps · Barbell/Dumbbell
Hypertrophy
Focus on FEELING the stretch deep in your hamstrings. This is a hinge, not a squat — push your hips back.
Keep the bar close to your body, spine neutral. Lower until you feel that deep hamstring pull, then drive up.
Sets3
Reps8–10
Rest90 sec
MusclesHamstrings, Glutes, Lower Back
05

Plank

3 sets · 45–60 second holds · Bodyweight
Core Prehab
Elbows directly under shoulders. Squeeze your glutes, brace your abs — body in a perfect straight line.
Don't let your hips sag or pike up. Quality over time — 30 perfect seconds beats 60 lazy ones.
Sets3
Hold45–60 seconds
Rest60 sec
MusclesCore, Glutes, Shoulders

DAY 2 — UPPER BODY

Push Power & Thick Back · Chest · Shoulders · Back · Arms

5 Exercises 55–65 min Push + Pull Balance

Goal: Upper body pressing explosiveness and back width.

Build the kind of upper body that can absorb contact in sports — wide lats, a powerful chest, and armor-plated arms. Start every session with the explosive movement first.

01

Plyometric Push-Ups (Clapping push-ups)

3 sets · 5 reps · Explosive
Explosive
If you can't clap yet, just push off the ground as hard as possible. Drop to knees if needed.
Land soft and absorb the impact — this protects your wrists and reloads power for the next rep.
Sets3
Reps5 (max power)
Rest60–90 sec
MusclesChest, Triceps, Shoulders
02

Dumbbell or Barbell Bench Press

4 sets · 6–8 reps · Barbell/Dumbbell
Main Strength
Retract your shoulder blades and arch your back slightly. Drive your feet into the floor.
Control the bar down to your lower chest, then press with power. Don't bounce off your chest.
Sets4
Reps6–8
Rest2 min
MusclesChest, Shoulders, Triceps
03

Single-Arm Dumbbell Row

3 sets · 10 reps per arm · Dumbbell
Hypertrophy

Reps10
Rest90 sec
MusclesLats, Rhomboids, Biceps
04

Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press

3 sets · 8–10 reps · Dumbbells
Hypertrophy
Sit upright with dumbbells at ear height. Press up and slightly in — don't lock out the elbows fully. Control the descent.

Arms

Tricep Pushdowns: Immediately after curls with no rest. Elbows locked at sides, push down and squeeze hard at the bottom.

DAY 3 — REST

Active Recovery · Mobility · Skill Work

Recovery Day 20–30 min optional

Sleep

Aim for 8–9 hours. Muscle is built in recovery, not in the gym. Protect your sleep like you protect your training.

Hydration

Minimum 3 litres of water. Add electrolytes if you sweat heavily. Dehydration kills performance.

Light Movement

Optional: 20–30 min walk, light stretching, foam rolling, or sport skill practice. Keep it easy.

Nutrition

Keep protein high (0.8–1g per lb bodyweight). Don't skip meals — rest days are when your muscles repair and grow.

DAY 4 — LOWER BODY

Hip Hinge & Sprint Power · Hamstrings · Glutes · Posterior Chain

5 Exercises 55–70 min Sprint Speed Builder

Goal: Build a bulletproof posterior chain for sprinting speed.

The hamstrings, glutes and lower back are your engine for speed. Kettlebell swings are the single best exercise for training the hip snap that drives sprint acceleration. Snap your hips forward violently.

01

Kettlebell Swings (or Broad Jumps)

3 sets · 8 reps (KB) / 4 reps (Broad Jumps) · Explosive
Explosive
Snap your hips forward violently at the top — this is the movement pattern that drives sprint speed. The arms are just ropes.
This is NOT a squat. Hinge at the hips, soft knees, and let the kettlebell float to shoulder height through hip power alone.
Sets3
Reps8 (KB) / 4 (Jumps)
Rest90 sec
MusclesGlutes, Hamstrings, Core
02

Trap Bar Deadlift (or Conventional Barbell Deadlift)

4 sets · 5 reps · Heavy
Main Strength
Lift heavy — but keep your back flat and spine neutral the entire rep. There is zero compromise on form.
Push the floor away from you rather than thinking about pulling the bar up. Squeeze your glutes at the top.
Sets4
Reps5 (heavy)
Rest3 min
MusclesHamstrings, Glutes, Back, Core
03

3 sets · 8–10 reps per leg · Bodyweight/Dumbbells
Hypertrophy
The king of athletic leg builders. Rear foot elevated on a bench. Front foot far enough forward that your shin stays vertical.
Embrace the burn — this exercise directly translates to single-leg power, speed, and balance on the field.

Reps8–10
Rest2 min

DAY 5 — UPPER BODY

Pull Power & Shoulders · Back · Chest · Shoulder Health

5 Exercises 55–65 min Armor-Plated

Goal: Build a wide, armor-plated upper body and explosive pulling power.

Medicine ball slams train the explosive overhead-to-ground power pattern. Pull-ups build the wide lats that give you that V-taper. Face pulls protect your shoulders from injury.

01

Medicine Ball Slams

3 sets · 8 reps · Explosive Full Body
Explosive
Reach HIGH overhead, then slam the ball into the floor as hard as humanly possible. Roar inside if you need to.
Use a dead rubber ball (not bouncy). Engage your lats on the overhead reach — this protects your lower back.

4 sets · 6–8 reps · Bodyweight
Main Strength
Dead hang at the bottom, chin over the bar at the top. No kipping. If you can't do 6, use an assistance band or switch to lat pulldowns.
Pull-ups are the single best upper body exercise for building a wide, powerful back. Own this movement.

DAY 6 — ATHLETIC ENGINE

Optional · Conditioning · Speed · Vanity Muscle

3 Exercises 30–45 min No Heavy Lifts

Optional Day — Conditioning, Speed & Vanity

No heavy barbell lifts today. This session builds your athletic engine — the conditioning that lets you perform in the 4th quarter when others fade. Sprint, move, and finish with calves and grip.

01

30-Meter All-Out Sprints

6–8 sets · Walk back to recover · Full Speed
Sprints
Sprint at ABSOLUTE maximum velocity — not 90%, not 95%. 100%. Every single rep.
Walk slowly back to the start after each sprint. This is your full recovery. Never sacrifice quality for quantity.
Stop when your sprint times start dropping noticeably — quality over quantity always wins here.
Sets6–8
Distance30 meters
RestWalk back (full recovery)
MusclesGlutes, Hamstrings, Quads, Calves
02

Cone Drills or Ladder Footwork

10 minutes continuous · Agility & Coordination
Agility
Sport-specific movement patterns: 5-10-5 shuffle, T-drill, or agility ladder if available.
Focus on sharp cuts, change of direction speed, and body control. This directly transfers to any sport.
Duration10 minutes
FormatContinuous movement
RestOnly when needed
FocusChange of Direction Speed

3 rounds · Calf Raises + 60 sec Farmer's Walk
Vanity
Calf Raises: 3 sets × 20 reps on a step edge, full range of motion. Pause at the top for a 1-second contraction.
Farmer's Walks: Immediately grab heavy dumbbells and walk for 60 seconds. Builds grip, traps, core stability and mental toughness.

🧬 The 3 Laws of This Program

01

Progressive Overload

To gain muscle, you must force adaptation. Every week, try to add 5 lbs to your main lifts, or do 1 more rep than last week. Track everything.

02

Explosive Intent

When doing the first exercise each day (jumps, slams, plyos), rest 60–90 seconds between sets. You must be at 100% maximum speed for every single rep.

03

Fuel the Beast

You will not gain a pound of muscle without a caloric surplus and at least 0.8–1 gram of protein per pound of bodyweight, daily. Sleep 8 hours minimum.
