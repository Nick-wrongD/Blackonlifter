DELOAD PROTOCOL — Every 4–6 Weeks

Signs you need a deload: persistent joint pain, stalled PRs for 2+ weeks, disrupted sleep, loss of motivation, reduced bar speed. When these hit, take a 7-day deload: drop volume by 40–50%, keep intensity at RPE 6–7, prioritize sleep and nutrition recovery.

REST DAYS

Day 6 & 7 — Active Recovery & Adaptation

2 days off Adaptation Phase

SLEEP PROTOCOL

8–9 hours minimum. Your muscles grow during sleep, not during training. Growth hormone peaks in deep sleep cycles. Blackout curtains, 67°F room, no screens 1 hour before bed.

NUTRITION ON REST DAYS

Slightly reduce carbohydrates (–15%) on rest days. Maintain high protein intake (1g per lb bodyweight). Prioritize micronutrients: leafy greens, zinc, magnesium, omega-3s.

ACTIVE RECOVERY

20–30 min low-intensity walk, swim, or bike. Light yoga or mobility work. Foam rolling trigger points: IT band, thoracic spine, lats, hip flexors. Nothing that elevates heart rate above 130 BPM.

DELOAD INDICATORS

Track these signs every week: bar speed declining over 2+ sessions, persistent joint pain, grip strength reduction, sleep quality dropping, motivation near zero. Any 3 of these = schedule a deload week immediately.

HYDRATION

Minimum 3–4 liters of water daily. Add electrolytes if sweating heavily. Dehydration as little as 2% can reduce strength by 10–15%. Creatine monohydrate 5g/day, any time.

MENTAL RECOVERY

Psychological fatigue is real. Use rest days to disconnect — no training videos, no macro-tracking obsession. Journal progress. Visualize next week's sessions. Stress management directly impacts cortisol and recovery.
