REST & RECOVERY

Days 4–5 · Active Recovery Optional

Sleep

Aim for 8–9 hours. Growth happens during recovery, not during the session.

Hydration

Minimum 3–4 litres of water. Add electrolytes if training intensity is high.

Nutrition

Keep protein high (1.8–2.2g per kg bodyweight). Don't skip meals on rest days.

Active Recovery

Optional: 20–30 min walk, light stretching, foam rolling, or swimming.

Contrast Method · Compound Power · Accessory Work

10 Exercises 75–90 min Max Output

Contrast Method Training

Each main lift is paired with an explosive movement. Heavy strength set → 30-second rest → explosive drop set. This activates Post-Activation Potentiation (PAP). All weighted movements follow a 5RM build-up protocol.
